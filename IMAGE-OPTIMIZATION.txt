AB Media automatic image optimization

What changed:
- Decap CMS can keep uploading original photos to images/uploads.
- Every Netlify production build runs scripts/optimize-images.js.
- The build creates WebP versions at 480px, 960px, and 1600px widths.
- Gallery, cover, and headshot images use responsive srcset/sizes so phones download smaller files than desktops.
- Gallery images lazy-load and decode asynchronously.
- If an optimized derivative is unavailable, the browser falls back to the original upload.

Important:
- Keep package.json, package-lock.json (after Netlify/npm creates it), netlify.toml, scripts/optimize-images.js, and the updated js/site.js in the repository.
- Do not manually upload files into images/optimized. Netlify regenerates that folder during builds.
- Original uploads remain in GitHub/Decap; visitors normally receive the smaller generated WebP versions.

Appearance:
- Decap Website Content now includes a Website Appearance > Theme control.
- Dark is the default; Light switches the public site to a white background with black text.
